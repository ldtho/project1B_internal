Guide

Viewing

  • Pick an episode in the list. ☰ shows / hides it; drag its right edge to resize it, and below the minimum it hides.
  • Use the video size slider above the player to shrink or enlarge its display between 25% and 100%. The size is remembered across episodes and reloads; fullscreen uses the full screen.
  • The timeline has one lane per level: 1 instruction, 2 sub-task (where available), 3 atomic. Click a caption to select it and jump to its start; click or drag the ruler to scrub; − / fit / + (left of the ruler) zoom, down to 0.1 s ticks.
  • The selected annotation follows the playhead: atomic caption, then sub-task, then instruction when timed captions are absent. Use selected annotation above the video to show or hide this area. Edits update it, the timeline, and video captions together. Qwen's check notes show flagged captions; the table below lists every caption under its sub-task.

Filters and flags

  • Combine dataset, split, task, scene, annotation-level and flag filters to narrow the episode list. L2 only, L3.5 only and L2 + L3.5 (both) refer to captions actually available in the episode.
  • Scene and Flags controls appear only when the selected dataset provides that metadata. Options update when you switch datasets; unavailable selections clear automatically. Annotation-level tags and L2 source badges appear on episode cards.
  • EgoExoLearn shows recordings with at least 80% fine-caption coverage after selection filters. Each video belongs to one train, validation or test split. Source split labels in metadata preserve native action-level labels.
  • all flags applies no flag restriction; any flag selects episodes with at least one flag; no flags selects episodes without recorded flags. Flags are separate from QA review status.
  • annotation check: Qwen judged the instruction or a sub-task wrong. The check note shows original and suggested text.
  • partial split: a sub-task had no accepted atomic split, so its own text stands in as an atomic line. This and annotation check use the Flags dropdown; there are no separate toggles.
  • review notes: Pantheon correction notes are available. They may describe wording changes rather than confirmed errors; expand Review notes on the episode to read them.
  • overlap conflict: incompatible hand events overlap in the original annotations. unannotated span: an interval had no usable action annotation.
  • nonpositive event: a source event ends at or before its start. sub tick event: a source event disappears after clipping and rounding to the 0.1 s import grid.
  • missing subtask labels: the source contains a missing-task-text placeholder. It remains in the original source table, but is excluded from L2 playback captions.

Editing (the Edit mode switch, on by default)

  • Text: type in the box above the timeline, double-click the caption on the timeline (Enter keeps, Esc drops), or use the table. ✓ copies Qwen's suggested text into a flagged line.
  • Times: drag a caption to move it, its edges to resize, on the 0.1 s grid. A caption stops at its neighbours; move shared boundaries makes a touching neighbour follow instead, and magnet pulls an edge onto the playhead. In the table: m:ss.s or seconds, ⇤ / ⇥ = playhead, ↑ / ↓ ±0.1 s.
  • + Add caption fills the gap after the selected caption. It is disabled while the next caption touches it: shorten one first. × Delete leaves a gap and selects the caption before, so Add refills it. ✂ (table) splits at the playhead.
  • ⇆ Merge joins the selected caption with the next one, texts included. Duplicated hand tags are outlined red until each hand has one clause.
  • Sub-tasks may leave gaps; Pantheon task segments may also overlap. Other sub-task tracks must not overlap. Atomic lines must cover the video from 0:00.0 to its end without gaps, labelled [left hand] / [right hand] / [both hands] and joined with |; a save is refused until they do.
  • save logs the edit with your login name (the manifest is never touched), discard drops the unsaved changes, ↺ reset (next to the switch, asks first) drops every edit on the episode, saved and unsaved, back to the original captions. Episodes not assigned to you open view-only, without the switch. Check both the sub-tasks (L2) and the atomic lines (L3.5), then save: with a change the episode turns green, unchanged (save · no change) orange.
